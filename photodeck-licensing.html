Key Conditions
How pricing works
Price scales with reach and exclusivity, not the image itself — a single named publication costs less than a national campaign, and shared use costs less than exclusive use.
How long it lasts
Unless stated otherwise above, licences must be exercised within 12 months of purchase. This is a window for making use of the licence, not a deadline for removing material already published within it. Renewal is available at the same rate, subject to review.
Updated photographs
If a building or location is re-photographed at a later date, the new photographs are issued under a new licence. Existing licences cover only the images purchased at the time.
Passing it on
No licence permits passing it on to a third party unless agreed in writing. All licences are issued to the purchasing entity or named individual only.
Copyright enforcement
Unlicensed use is followed up on a case-by-case basis. The Single Listing Use rate is used as the reference price for basic copyright claims. Advertising or campaign use without a licence is assessed at the applicable commercial rate.
