Key Conditions
How pricing works
Price scales with reach and exclusivity, not the image itself — shared use costs less than exclusive use.
Duration
Licences must be exercised within 12 months of purchase (a window for making use, not for removing already published material).
Updated photographs
Re-photographed locations at a later date require a new licence. Existing licences cover only images purchased at the time.
Transferability
No licence permits passing rights to a third party unless agreed in writing. Issued to the purchasing entity only.
Copyright enforcement
Unlicensed use is billed using the Single Listing Use rate as a reference, or commercial rates for major advertising.
AI & Machine Learning
Images, metadata, and coordinates may not be scraped, bulk downloaded, or used to train AI/ML systems under any licence.
